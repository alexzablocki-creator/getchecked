The rules

Simple and fair

No surprises. No fine print.

💰
You keep 100%
GetChecked never takes a cut of your revenue. Pilot stations pay $0. Post-pilot is a flat annual license.
📵
No cancellations
Customers can't cancel once booked unless your station is closed that day. No-shows are non-refundable — you keep the payment.
🛡️
Item insurance
GetChecked provides item protection insurance covering customer belongings during storage. You are not liable for item loss.
📱
No app needed
Your dashboard is a bookmark on your phone at getchecked.co/dashboard/[your ID]. No download, no login hassle.
🚪
Cancel anytime
Pilot stations can leave anytime. No contracts. We just ask for 7 days notice so we can notify any upcoming bookings.
💚
5% Stub
Pledge 5% of your storage revenue to a neighborhood cause you choose. We display it on your station page — good for your community and your reputation.
